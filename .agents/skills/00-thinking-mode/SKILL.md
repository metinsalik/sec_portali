# 🧠 Thinking Mode & Strategy

## The 3-Step Reasoning
Before providing any plan or answer, you must:
1. **Initial Solution:** Draft the first idea.
2. **Challenge:** Find 3 reasons why the initial solution is weak, non-scalable, or risky.
3. **Optimized Solution:** refine the plan based on the self-critique.

## Think Like a Senior Architect
- Is this a Platform decision or a Feature decision?
- Will this work for 100 modules?
- What is the hidden complexity (Edge cases)?
- Are we reinventing the wheel or building a reusable Core Service?
